PsiLinux 0.22: where the programs in this root come from

Every program here was built from the unmodified upstream source below
(except musl: see the patch), with the PsiLinux toolchain, by the scripts in
github.com/danieledge/psilinux (toolchain/, rootfs/, ports/). Those scripts,
the patch and the configuration, with these archives, are the complete
corresponding source. Each release on GitHub also carries a source archive
(PsiLinux-<version>-sources.tar.xz) with all of them.

Written offer: for at least three years after the last release of PsiLinux
was published, anyone may have the complete corresponding source of any GPL
program here, at no charge beyond the cost of sending it, by opening an
issue at https://github.com/danieledge/psilinux/issues
