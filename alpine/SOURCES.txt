PsiLinux's Alpine root (Alpine Linux 3.24.2, armhf): where its programs come from

Every package here is Alpine Linux's own signed binary package, unmodified,
installed by Alpine's apk. PsiLinux adds only its own small files
(github.com/danieledge/psilinux, folder alpine/overlay/, MIT licence).

The source of each package is Alpine's recipe for it (the APKBUILD and its
patches, in aports, https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports, branch
3.24-stable, at the commit that built the version below: its "origin" is the
folder) and the upstream archive the recipe names. Each PsiLinux release on
GitHub also carries a source archive (PsiLinux-<version>-sources.tar.xz) with
the recipes and archives of the copyleft packages below.

Written offer: for at least three years after the last release of PsiLinux
was published, anyone may have the complete corresponding source of any GPL
or LGPL package here, at no charge beyond the cost of sending it, by opening
an issue at https://github.com/danieledge/psilinux/issues

The GPL and LGPL texts are beside this file (GPL-2.0.txt, GPL-3.0.txt,
LGPL-2.1.txt). bash and nano, for example, are GPL v3 or later.

Packages installed later with apk come from Alpine's mirror, not from
PsiLinux; their source is in aports in the same way.
