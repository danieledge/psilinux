PsiLinux: what next

The desktop works: psiwm draws everything in the Psion's own style, with one copy of Xlib for the window manager and all its programs. Memory is the thing to watch: X takes about 5 MB, which leaves room for a terminal, Files and Notes at once.

To do this week:

- try Links in graphics mode

Ideas: a Data-style address book, an Agenda that reads the Psion's own files, sound through the speaker.
